# Specification: Scene Enrichment — Ground Plane, Skybox & Camera Cylinder

## Summary

Add a ground plane beneath the rotating meshes, a light blue sky background color, and a cylinder primitive positioned at the orbiting camera and pointing in its forward direction to serve as a "flashlight" for lighting verification.

## Context

Phase 15 added Phong lighting with ambient, diffuse, and specular shading. The current demo scene has a cube and tetrahedron floating in dark grey space. To better visualize and manually test the lighting pipeline, the scene needs more geometry (a ground plane to receive shadows-like shading, a cylinder to track the camera) and a sky-colored background so the scene feels spatially grounded.

## User Story

As a learner studying shading, I want a richer demo scene with a ground plane, skybox color, and camera-following cylinder so that I can see how Phong shading interacts with different surfaces and light positions.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Ground plane visible beneath meshes with correct Phong lighting (lit face bright, underside dark)
- [ ] Light blue skybox background color (clear color change, not a cubemap texture)
- [ ] `NewCylinder()` primitive in `pkg/geometry` with per-face normals
- [ ] Cylinder positioned at the orbiting camera, oriented along the camera's forward direction
- [ ] All new geometry (plane, cylinder) receives Phong shading correctly

## Dependencies

- Phase 15 (Phong lighting, normals, uniform buffers)

## Out of Scope

- Actual skybox cubemap texture — just a background clear color change
- Shadow casting from cylinder or plane
- Texture mapping on any geometry
- Point/spot lights (directional only)

## Technical Notes

- Ground plane: a large flat quad (2 triangles) with normal pointing up (+Y), positioned at Y = -1 or similar
- Cylinder: parametric mesh with configurable segment count; lateral faces have outward radial normals, caps have +Y/-Y normals
- Camera cylinder: compute position and rotation from camera position + forward vector each frame, update transform in the draw loop
- Background clear color: update `wgpu.Color` in `RenderFrameMulti`
